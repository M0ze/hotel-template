Program

Independent hospitality, rooted in Mubende

Joyce Hotel Mubende opened in 2018 as a quiet answer to the growing appetite for slower travel in central Uganda. It is a small property — twenty-four rooms, three dining rooms, one wellness house — and what it lacks in scale it makes up for in attention.

Every guest is met by the same concierge team from arrival to departure. Meals are seasonal and local; the gardens are kept organic; the rooms are set only when you ask. We do not chase volume. We build a place that holds space for the way people choose to travel now.
